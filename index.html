Calendar Bridge

A small, self-hosted service that keeps one person's iCloud calendar and Google Calendar in sync, so the same events are available in both.

Calendar Bridge is a personal tool, not a public service. It runs on its operator's own machine and only accesses the operator's own Google and Apple accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.

What it does

  • Copies events in both directions between an iCloud calendar and a Google calendar that the operator owns.
  • Keeps both copies consistent when an event changes. Conflicting edits are never overwritten silently; they wait for the operator to decide.
  • Shows its synchronization status in a separate "Calendar Bridge Status" calendar in the same Google account.

Google permissions it requests

  • See the list of your calendars (calendar.calendarlist.readonly).
  • See, create, change and delete events on calendars you own (calendar.events.owned).
  • Create its own secondary calendars and manage the events in them (calendar.app.created).

It never sends invitations or updates to other people.
